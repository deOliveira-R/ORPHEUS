## 2. The one new home for the reason — `sn-boundary-gs-not-regular`

Per the brief ("point at the canonical warning rather than restate the
derivation"), the derivation lives in **exactly one** doc place, and the other
eight sites `:ref:` it. New H3 section in
`docs/theory/methods/sn/cartesian_multid.rst`, inserted immediately before
`.. _sn-boundary-gs-rate-regime:` so the obstruction sets up the measured table
that follows:

`.. _sn-boundary-gs-not-regular:` — *"Why it is a splitting but not a regular
splitting"*, with three H4 subsections:

1. **The statement and what it would license.** Varga's definition
   (`M⁻¹ ≥ 0` **and** `N ≥ 0`), the comparison theorem as a bare `.. math::`,
   and the observation that `N_J − N_GS = B_lower ≥ 0` makes it *exactly* the
   claim "boundary G-S is never slower than Jacobi". Then the measured
   contradiction (1.95× slower at d=3 vs 2.5× faster at d=2, same solver).
2. **The obstruction, in the limit where it is exact.** Zero leakage; `B`'s
   octant action is `Q_d`, *a permutation, hence non-negative* — the negativity
   is one level down, inside the octant. New labelled equation
   `dd-face-transmission-spectrum`:
   `Σ_{a←b} = 2w_b/D − δ_ab`, i.e. `Σ = (2/D)·1wᵀ − I`, with `w_a = 2|μ_a|A_a`
   and `D = Σ_t V + Σ_b w_b`; spectrum as a `list-table`
   (`1 − 2Σ_tV/D` on `1`, multiplicity 1; `−1` on `{wᵀv = 0}`, multiplicity
   `d−1`). Closes with the multiplicity point: `d = 1` has **no** such mode,
   which is why the 1-D Jacobi fallback was never a rate question and why the
   pathology could not appear before the schedule reached multi-D.
3. **The undamped subspace is a property of the DIAMOND closure.** Same
   construction under step differencing gives
   `Σ_step = (1/D')·1w'ᵀ` — rank one, **no** `−I`, spectrum
   `{(D'−Σ_tV)/D'} ∪ {0}^{d−1}`: the same `d−1` modes *maximally damped
   instead of undamped*. DD's second-order accuracy and its undamped face
   sawtooth are one property seen twice. Then the tie to #340's measured mode,
   with its full configuration, and the derivation of *why* the two trace
   ratios sum to exactly 2 (`ψ_in + ψ_out = 2ψ_c` **is** the closure).
4. **What survives, and what this does not explain.** Splitting valid; drivers
   unchanged; fixed point splitting-invariant (Mode 9 gate named); Hackbusch
   §11 untouched; row-split law and the ERR-056 fan-in rule independent of
   regularity. Then the honest-scope clause: **the obstruction makes an
   inversion *possible*; it does not predict which side** — "read it as
   'nothing bounds it either way'".

### Verification of the algebra I published

I re-derived and numerically checked both spectra rather than transcribing
them (`d ∈ {2,3,4}`, several `w`, `Σ_t`, `V`): DD eigenvalues match
`{−1}^{d−1} ∪ {1 − 2Σ_tV/D}` to `1e-12`; step eigenvalues match
`{0}^{d−1} ∪ {(D'−Σ_tV)/D'}`. The `1.074414 / 0.925586` pair is quoted with
its source (`scratch/d3_absorber_diagnosis.md` §4) and with the *structural*
reason it sums to 2, so the number is self-evidencing rather than trusted.

## 6. ⚠ A discrepancy the main agent should see (does NOT affect the corpus)

`scratch/issue_341_gs_jacobi_mechanism.md` §6.1/§6.2 argues `ndim` is not the
discriminating variable using **ρ-derived** ratios (`n_GS/n_J = ln ρ_J / ln
ρ_GS`) from the ARPACK instrument. I re-measured five of those rows as **actual
SI sweep counts** — the quantity the published tables report — matching each
row's `Σ_t` by using a *uniform* two-group absorber (a pure absorber decouples
the groups exactly, so this is two identical copies of the memo's 1-group
problem):

| memo row | memo `n_GS/n_J` (from ρ) | my sweep counts | ratio | sign |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| d=3 (2,4,8) ext(1,2,4) Σ_t 0.8 LS4 | 0.576 | 1499 / 599 | 2.503 | **DIFFERS** |
| d=3 (3,3,3) ext(1,1,1) Σ_t 0.05 LS4 | 0.787 | 12001 / 3788 | 3.168 | **DIFFERS** |
| d=3 (3,4,5) ext(1,2,3) Σ_t 0.8 LS2 | 0.919 | 792 / 765 | 1.035 | **DIFFERS** |
| d=2 (2,2) ext(6,6) Σ_t 2.0 LS4 | 1.146 | 62 / 60 | 1.033 | agrees |
| d=2 (3,3) ext(1,1) Σ_t 51.2 LS4 | 1.039 | 106 / 108 | 0.981 | **DIFFERS** |

Both instruments are individually sound — the memo's §1.2 control validates ρ
against a fitted residual decay to 4 decimals, and my CTRL row reproduces
1631/838 exactly. The plausible reading is that when `ρ_GS ≈ ρ_J` to within a
fraction of a percent, the *asymptotic* rate ratio `ln ρ_J / ln ρ_GS` is
enormously sensitive while the *sweep count* is dominated by the transient and
the residual constant (and, per the memo's own §1.3, by a frozen null-space
component the stopping test cannot see).

**Consequence for the memo's conclusion:** the conclusion survives — `ndim` is
not the discriminating variable — because my own d=2 sweep-count
counterexamples establish it independently. What does **not** survive
unqualified is the specific "d=3 fixtures where G-S WINS" list, which does not
reproduce in sweep counts. I published only the d=2 side.

**Recommendation:** before any of §6/§8/§11 of that memo is published or acted
on, decide which observable the argument is about (asymptotic ρ or sweeps to
tolerance) and state it per table. They are not interchangeable in this regime.
