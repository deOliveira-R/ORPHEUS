## Candidate — M1: construct the subject and measure before designing the gate

**Digest source:** `lessons.proposed.md` §0, meta-lesson M1 — the
most-evidenced of the eight meta-lessons (13 archive pointers: `L41b`,
`L43h`, `L58f`, `L67a`, `L77a`, `L77f`, `L79b`, `L80f`, `L81c`, `L83a`,
`L83c`, `L84i`, `L86d`), spanning nine distinct campaigns from 2026-08-06
through 2026-09-17. Its own text: "Half of all findings are that the gate is
unwritable, vacuous, or already true" — a claim about the AGENT's own
output quality across the whole memory, not one campaign's incident.

**Why AGENT.md and not a rule uplift:** this is a WORKFLOW step specific to
how test-architect drafts a plan (what to do before §1.5's claim-layer
classification), not a general evidentiary law — `instrument-doctrine` X1
already states the general law this operationalises ("an instrument is
evidence only if some realizable state changes its reading... name the
input that would make it read differently"). AGENT.md is where this agent's
own operational form of a general law belongs; see `uplift.md` for the two
candidates that ARE proposed as rule clauses instead.

**Proposed insertion — a new item at the head of §1.5's numbered list**
(before "1. Claim layer."), renumbering the existing three to 2–4:

> 1. **Construct and measure first.** Before naming a claim layer or a
>    pillar, build the object the gate will assert on, print the field the
>    gate will read, evaluate the dispatch predicate on a PRODUCTION
>    instance (not a hand-built stand-in), and probe the FACE the consumer
>    actually reads. Do this before reading the design further and before
>    drafting a single row of the matrix. A design read but not measured is
>    a hypothesis about what the gate will find, not a plan.

**What to leave in the digest:** M1's own line in `lessons.proposed.md` §0
gains a `→ now in AGENT.md §1.5` pointer alongside its existing `→ LNN`
archive pointers; the meta-lesson stays (it is the evidence trail for why
the directive exists, and its 13 pointers are the census a future session
would want when deciding whether the directive is still earning its place).
