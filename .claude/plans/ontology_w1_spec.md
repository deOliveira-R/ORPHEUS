## 2. The algebra in symbols

Notation. Real finite-dimensional spaces. `I` is an ordered finite index set; `R^I` its coordinate space with the Euclidean pairing `<.,.>`. `V*` is the dual. For a symmetric positive semidefinite `G` on `R^I` write `ker G` for its null space. A *declaration* `d` of `R^I` is a subset description (2.3); `Ann d` is its annihilator in `(R^I)*`, identified with `R^I` by the Euclidean pairing, so `Ann Coord(J) = Coord(I minus J)` and `Ann Ann d = d`.

### 2.1 Objects

**S1 Index set and layout.** `I` finite and ordered. Product `I x J` (row-major) and coproduct `I (+) J` (offsets `0, |I|`). A `Layout` is an index set with its flat order and product and coproduct (concept `layout`).

**S2 Form.** A form on `I` is one of: `Absent` (no reference measure: no density, no flat, no sharp, no norm); `Diag(w)` with `w` in `R^I`; `Dense(G)` with `G` symmetric. `Diag(1)` (counting) is a form and is not `Absent`. Each present form has a signature `(n_+, n_0, n_-)` and a kernel `ker G`. `Diag` reads both exactly from the signs of `w`; for a `Dense` form the declared `kernel` implies them, `(n - kernel.dim, kernel.dim, 0)`: the constructor takes the kernel and no signature, so no negative direction is representable by the declaration, and the check that the Gram has none (a floating-point test, so with a tolerance) is the gate G01d (amendment A6; D7 names the signature as a constructor argument, and the kernel declaration carries the same information). A form with `n_- > 0` is refused (the Krein arm is named, not built, D7).

**S3 Axis.** `a = (label, I, form, origin, restriction)` plus derived `kind` (nodal or modal), `support`, `nodes`, `layout`. Identity: content digest of `(type, label, shape, form content, kind, support, nodes, restriction)`; `origin` is provenance and not identity (as today, `numerics/axis.py:461`); `restriction` is `None` except on a restricted axis, where it records the parent axis's identity key and the index subset, so that a restricted axis is a NEW axis on the subset with its parent in its identity (amendment A2, 3.2). The axis carries no variance.

**S4 Variance.** `Variance = (kind, chart)` with `kind` in `{FUNCTION, MEASURE}` and `chart` in `{DENSITY, INTEGRATED}`. Kind is intrinsic to the index set (a function pulls back along an index map, a measure pushes forward; R16). The chart is the Radon-Nikodym identification of functions with measures, `f -> f w`, which exists only on an axis whose form is present. Two involutions on the four values: `dual(k, c) = (flip k, flip c)`, `riesz(k, c) = (k, flip c)`. They generate the Klein four-group `{id, dual, riesz, dual.riesz}` on `Variance` alone (4 values, no constraint).

**S5 Subquotient.** `sk = S/K` with declarations `K` inside `S` inside `Coord(I)`. The default is the whole space, `S = Coord(I)`, `K = Coord(empty)`. `dual` and `riesz` both send `S/K` to `(Ann K)/(Ann S)` (the coordinates are written in the stored chart; the Euclidean identification of `R^I` with its dual is the one fixed by S2's convention, A2 1b).

**Stored coordinates (amendment A2).** The stored coordinates of a factor are ALWAYS on the axis's full index set, whatever `S/K` is: a member of `S/K` is stored as a representative that vanishes off `S` and is free on `K`; a member of `(Ann K)/(Ann S)` vanishes on `K` and is free on `Ann S`. So `X.dual()` and `X.riesz(...)` keep `X`'s stored shape, and no stored array changes when the trace is typed (D21). The dimension of `S/K` is `dim S - dim K`. A view that stores only the rows of `S` is not a subquotient: it is a restricted axis (S3, 3.2).

**S6 Factor.** `f = (axis, variance, sk)`. The stored shape of `f` is the axis's shape for every `S/K` (the convention of S5, amendment A2); its dimension is `dim S - dim K`. For the whole space both equal `|I|`, unchanged from today (D11, D21).
- **Invariant A0** (energy law): if `axis.form` is `Absent` then `chart = DENSITY` iff `kind = FUNCTION`. On a formless axis kind and chart are one bit; `riesz` is therefore undefined there, and the Klein group there is `{id, dual}`.
- **Legality of `riesz`, stated once, on the density end (amendment A4).** A DENSITY factor is `riesz`-legal iff its axis has a form and `S ∩ ker G ⊆ K` (so the induced form of S7 is positive definite on `S/K`); an INTEGRATED factor is legal iff it is the `riesz` image of a legal density factor. Closure of the legal set under `dual` and `riesz` is then a consequence, not a second rule: `[R]` argued for `Coord` declarations on a coordinate kernel (a state with `S ∩ ker G ⊆ K` has `Ann K ∩ ker G ⊆ Ann S`), gated exhaustively by the 216-state enumeration of L3 and, for a `Spanned` kernel, by G03a's dense arm. This rewords the plan's item U1 (section 10).

**S7 Induced form.** The form of a DENSITY factor on `S/K` is the coset minimum: `q(x + K) = min_{k in K} (x+k)^T G (x+k)` on `S`, a Schur complement of `G` restricted to `S` by `K`. It is defined for every `S`, `K` (G semidefinite) and is nondegenerate exactly when `S ∩ ker G ⊆ K` `[M]` A2 B2: two sections of `V/span(1)` for a dense `G` differ by 11.0 while the coset minimum differs by 1.3e-15; the dual of `Sub(S)` with `G_SS` has the form `(G_SS)^-1`, the Schur complement of `G^-1`, matching to 2.2e-16 while the coordinate restriction `(G^-1)_SS` is off by 0.957. **The chart of the form (amendment A4):** on a DENSITY factor the form is the coset minimum `q` above; on an INTEGRATED factor it is the inverse of the induced form of its `riesz` partner (which is a density factor): for the dual of `Sub(S)` that is `(G_SS)^-1`, not the coordinate restriction. The form is therefore induced for every factor `Factor.__post_init__` accepts, on a `Coord`, a `Spanned` or an `Ann` declaration alike; a subquotient whose form cannot be induced is refused at construction.

**S8 Form blocks.** A *form home* of a space is a partition of its slot labels into form blocks. Each block has one form and, on `riesz`, one chart. W1 builds only singleton blocks, so chart is per slot on everything W1 builds. A block with more than one slot is named and is W3's (the curvilinear in-cell Gram is fibred over cells, not a Kronecker product: `[M]` A2 C2, Kronecker `sigma_2/sigma_1` = 3.3e-2, Cartesian 1.1e-16).

**S9 Tensor space.** `S = <f_1, ..., f_n>`, `n >= 0`, labels unique; as a vector space `(x)_i (R^{|S_i|})` with the factor's chart. The empty list is the unit `1 = R`.

**S10 Direct sum.** `D = (+)_l (label_l, S_l)` with one or more labelled summands (`[M]` a solid radial mesh has one traced face, `mesh/axis.py:393`, so the lower bound is 1). Summands may be direct sums. Labels are part of the identity.

**S11 The pair.** `TrialTest = (trial, test, testing)`: `trial` and `test` are spaces (a tensor space or a direct sum of them, recursing on the sum tree), `testing` maps each label to `NODE` or `INTEGRAL`; `NODE` requires a present form on that label's factor. On every label the kind of `trial` is opposite to the kind of `test`. `test` is a declared space (the test chart is not a function of the trial: the ordinate layout keeps the angular chart, the moment layout flips it, D15).

**S12 The pairing.** `DualPairing(left, right)` is an element of `(left (x) right)*`: the full contraction. Per factor the kinds must be opposite; per form block, charts opposite give the canonical contraction, both `DENSITY` give the form leg `G`, both `INTEGRATED` are refused (R15). Per factor the subquotients must match: the canonical leg requires `right.sk == left.sk.dual()`, the form leg requires `right.sk == left.sk`, and anything else is refused ("pairing of mismatched subquotients": a pairing whose value moves with the representative is unspellable).

### 2.3 The closed sums and their normal forms

## 6. The units

### 6.0 Conventions of this section

### 6.13 The predicate script (for a builder with no `scratch/`)

A unit's test call-site set is computed by this script; it parses with `ast` and counts a file when it contains the name as an `ast.Name`, an `ast.Attribute` attribute, an import alias, a keyword argument or a `def`/`class` name (a name only in a docstring, comment or string is excluded: prose-only files are edited for prose). Positive control `[M]`: run on `FullFieldSpace` at `b5f18d4e` it reads 15 production files and 20 test files, and on `DenseMetric` 4 and 21, equal to `measure_callsites.md` section 1 and to N01's set. It does not read string annotations (so a file naming a class only in an annotation string is not counted; `measure_callsites.md` counted those).

```python
import ast, pathlib, sys
def names(tree):
    out = set()
    for n in ast.walk(tree):
        if isinstance(n, ast.Name): out.add(n.id)
        elif isinstance(n, ast.Attribute): out.add(n.attr)
        elif isinstance(n, (ast.Import, ast.ImportFrom)): out |= {a.name.split(".")[-1] for a in n.names}
        elif isinstance(n, ast.keyword) and n.arg: out.add(n.arg)
        elif isinstance(n, (ast.FunctionDef, ast.AsyncFunctionDef, ast.ClassDef)): out.add(n.name)
    return out
def files(root, symbols):
    hits = []
    for p in pathlib.Path(root).rglob("*.py"):
        if "derivations" in p.parts and root == "orpheus": continue
        try: tree = ast.parse(p.read_text())
        except SyntaxError: continue
        if names(tree) & set(symbols): hits.append(str(p))
    return sorted(hits)
```
