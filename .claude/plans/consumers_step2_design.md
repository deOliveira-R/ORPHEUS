### 3.5 The pencil articulation — what a source-driven and an eigenvalue system each ARE, what is queried, and the type shape (`[R]` user's ask, 2026-09-13: *"consider the properties of source driven systems and what can be expressed as a pencil, the properties of eigenvalue systems expressed as a pencil. Then the commonalities between both and what makes them different (as in, what properties we might want to query as part of the problem expressed as a pencil), then you might want to plan this better. A single pencil that has or not a source or that has or not an eigenvalue might or might not be the best description of the problem depending on how we want to leverage the pencil expression."*)

**The source-driven system** (`[T]`/`[M]`): the affine system `Aψ = q`, `A ∈ Hom(V,V)`, `q ∈ V` — as a pencil, a ZERO-parameter pencil (`𝒜 ≡ A`, `Λ = {•}`); its non-degenerate faces are the subcritical multiplying source `(A − F)ψ = q` = `pencil.at(1)` WITH a source, and the Laplace form `(A + sT)ψ̂ = q̂(s) + Tψ₀` where the initial condition enters through the SOURCE. Solution set: a COSET `ψ₀ + ker A` (affine, not a ray). Well-posedness is the **Fredholm alternative** — TWO kernels govern two halves: `ker A` → uniqueness (the gauge; ships as `LossKernelGauge`, `[M]` ONE production reader `_exit_gauge_trace` at 5 sites, applied to the returned iterate), `ker A†` → solvability (`q ∈ range A = (ker A†)^⊥`; **absent** — nothing computes `ker A†`, nothing tests `q`; on an all-reflective σ_a = 0, Σ₂ = 0 box the condition is literally `⟨1_g, q⟩ = 0`, so a source problem with a net source and no sink has no steady solution, and the Problem could refuse it at posing time instead of exhausting a budget). Λ: `{•}`, or σ an INPUT. Superposition in `q`. The adjoint problem needs a DETECTOR `R` — extra data not in the generating data (`[M]` `solve_sn_adjoint_fixed_source` takes its own `q*`, `:3101`). Queries: the factors; `q` and its two blocks (bulk + prescribed inflow — `[M]` the affine BC term `γ₋ψ = RGγ₊ψ + q_b` is part of the SOURCE, `solver.py:3175`); `residual(ψ) = q − Aψ`; `balance(ψ) = ⟨1_g, Aψ − q⟩`; `dim ker A` + the gauge; `admits(q)`; the response `⟨R, ψ⟩ = ⟨ψ†, q⟩`.

**The eigenvalue system** (`[T]`/`[M]`): `Aψ = λMψ`; the object is the PAIR `(A, M)`; regular iff `det(A − λM) ≢ 0` — guaranteed here since `𝒜(0) = A` is what the sweep inverts; `M` rank-deficient (`F` a dyad, zero on non-fissile cells and on the ray system, `[M]` `numerics/coupled_system.py:1382`) ⟹ Weierstrass–Kronecker: exactly `rank M` finite eigenvalues — the k-problem IS an `r`-dimensional eigenproblem on `range F` (the fission-matrix fact; 0-D: `r = 1`, `k_∞ = trace(A⁻¹F) = ⟨νΣ_f, A⁻¹χ⟩`, a closed form). Solution set: a RAY in `P(K)` (Krein–Rutman: the unique cone eigenvector) PLUS a scalar. Spectrum = the POLES of `R(σ) = (A − σM)⁻¹`; Λ = ℂ and λ is an OUTPUT. `k` always exists; α has a continuous part (admissibility `Σ_t − σ/v > 0`). The adjoint pencil `(A†, M†)` shares the spectrum, biorthogonality through `M` — NO extra datum (`k† = k`, `[M]` `eigenvalue.py:31-33`). Queries: the pair; `at(σ)`; `is_regular`; `rhs_rank`; the spectral map; `rayleigh(ψ, w) = ⟨w, Aψ⟩/⟨w, Mψ⟩` (`compute_keff` is the `w = 1` member; `w = ψ†` the stationary one); the eigen-residual; Collatz–Wielandt brackets; the dominance ratio; the NORMALIZATION gauge (owed — `[M]` `homogeneous/solver.py:414` applies `phi * (100/rate)` and records nothing; `SolutionBase` has no gauge field).

**Commonalities:** one `V, G, K`; one factor set; `𝒜(σ)` affine in σ (`at` linear, `at(0) = A`); `.H` determined by `(𝒜, G)`; a residual; ONE balance functional `β(ψ, λ) = ⟨1_g, 𝒜(λ)ψ − q⟩` (the eigen kind SOLVES `β = 0` for λ — `compute_keff`; the source kind EVALUATES it — `_exit_balance_defect`; `[M]` spelled twice today); one resolvent consumes both.
